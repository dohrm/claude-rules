# Search choice

A campaign editor searches about 5,000 markdown notes on a laptop. Its lexical search misses synonyms. The product team is revisiting its search architecture and has not supplied a current availability or data-handling policy in this brief. The search-memory budget is 1.5 GB. No quality or latency benchmark for semantic search exists yet.

A contributor proposes moving campaign-note embeddings and vector queries to a hosted search service. Compare that option with improving local lexical retrieval and other credible approaches. Give the proposal its strongest case, costs and unknowns, then make a provisional recommendation.
