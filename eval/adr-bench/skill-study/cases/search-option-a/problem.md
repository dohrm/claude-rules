# Search choice

A local-first campaign editor searches about 5,000 markdown notes on a laptop. Its lexical search misses synonyms. Search must remain usable offline, and campaign text must not leave the device. The search-memory budget is 1.5 GB. No quality or latency benchmark for semantic search exists yet.

A contributor proposes an in-process semantic index packaged with the application, without a separate service; embeddings would be computed on the laptop. Compare that option with improving lexical retrieval and other credible approaches. Give the proposal its strongest case, costs and unknowns, then make a provisional recommendation.
