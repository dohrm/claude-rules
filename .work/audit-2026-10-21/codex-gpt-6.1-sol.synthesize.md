## Disputed

No review records a `disagree` verdict. Agreement includes these proposal qualifications:

- **autonomy.skills.defect-diagnosis-fix-authority-conflict:** Automatic fixing at L3 was offered as an alternative. The explicit invocation contract at `skills/defect/SKILL.md:10–13`, `:40–42` and `:135` is stronger evidence: preserve diagnosis-only behavior.
- **autonomy.installer.multiple-module-root-is-not-aggregation:** A wording correction is cheaper, but leaves generated gates without established module coverage (`bin/cli.mjs:894–897`). Keep the M estimate for the behavioral fix.
- **autonomy.eval.no-case-for-execution-path-skills:** The proposed wildcard and absence assertions exceed current artifact support (`eval/run.mjs:248–254`, `:283–285`). Use concrete paths and explicit preservation checks; extend assertions where required.
- **cost.rules.autonomy-l4-always-on-and-restated:** Consolidation is supported by duplicated text. Guaranteed behavioral equivalence after shortening is unproven, and agent rules can be installed without RFC skills (`registry.json:25–30`, `:118–132`). Keep authority constraints independently available.
